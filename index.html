A Word from Our Strategic Coordinator

Grace and peace to you in our Lord Jesus.

As I look back at what our Lord has done in and through the Singapore church, I think of the original church at Antioch. That church was noted for how it sent the first long-term cross-cultural missionaries – Paul and Barnabas – and thus participated in God’s global mission.

But the Antioch church also stood out for how it submitted itself to the lordship of Christ – as its leaders gave themselves to worship, prayer and fasting (Acts 13:2-3), and obeyed the Spirit’s leading and released their best people for missions.

The Singapore church has long been inspired by the Antioch church – and that remains needful in our time today as the task that God has given His church remains unfinished.

Antioch21 was set up for this purpose – to serve the Singapore church to live out its Antioch calling – to send out the ones whom God has set apart for His service, so that the world may know that Jesus is Saviour and Lord, and that there be flourishing communities of Jesus followers where Christ is not known.

As Antioch21 enters the fourth year in the Decade of Missions (2023 – 2033), I look back and honour the heritage of those who had gone before us and who laid the foundations for our work. People like Pastor Rick Seaward and Joseph Chean who got the work of Antioch21 going.

I think back also on the last 3 years – the labour in the Lord of all who have served together in the Decade of Missions, and the gospel partnership that has stretched across so many churches and organisations.

Truly many streams, one direction.

This report describes what the Lord has been doing through our service. All that you will read about is a testimony of God at work through His servants. I am filled with gratitude for them all. I hope that you too will be greatly encouraged.

I also hope that you will see that much remains to be done. May the Lord speak to you through this report, and stir your heart for His glory among the nations.

I invite you to prayerfully partner with us in this Antioch journey.

So that “those who have never been told of him will see, and those who have never heard will understand.”

Romans 15:21

Unto His return.

Your fellow servant and partner in the gospel,

Reverend Ng Zhi-WenStrategic Coordinator, Antioch21

Who We Are

Antioch21 is a national missions movement relaunched on 25 Nov 2021 under the leadership of the late Joseph Chean, with roots reaching back to its inception in 2003 by the late Pastor Rick Seaward.

Our calling is to raise a missionary force of all generations, within this decade, to be launched into the gateway cities and least reached places, to reach the lost, the unreached and the unengaged people groups in Asia and beyond.

Our Faith Goals for 2033

80%Evangelical churches mobilised for missions
1,000 + 300Singapore and migrant missionaries placed (min. 2 years)
500New multiplying churches planted
50Unreached people groups adopted/engaged
100Wholistic mission initiatives pioneered
$10 millionCollective contributions raised for the deployment and care of missionaries

These goals were set not with the intent of ‘chasing after numbers’, but to guide the work that we do as a body of Christ. Antioch21 plans to undertake a review study in 2027 to more comprehensively understand the progress the church of Singapore has made in becoming a strong missions sending church.

Year by Year
2021
  • Official launch of Antioch21 on 25 November 2021.
2022
  • Laying the foundations.
2023
  • First Antioch Summit at ACS (Barker Road).
  • Decade of Missions and Antioch21 purpose statement officially launched.
  • 1361 Training launched.
2024
  • Formation of Core Team and Board.
  • Antioch Summit 2024 held at St. John’s-St. Margaret’s Church.
  • Church Missions Readiness Assessment (CMRA) tool launched, providing churches with a way to assess how prepared they are for missions, and receive suggestions on how to be more effective participants in local and global missions.
  • The Learning Journey was launched: Monthly training and equipping sessions for church missions leaders and committees.
2025
  • Incorporation of Antioch21 as a Company Limited by Guarantee.
  • Antioch Summit 2025 held at Bartley Christian Church (1,132 registered, 181 churches, 58% first-timers).
  • Missions Seed Fund launched. A pooled fund that local churches can apply to for partial financial support for missionaries in the early stages of their missions deployment journey.
  • Missions Training & Education Prospectus launched. Wherever a potential missions-goer is at – be it the discovery, discerning, deployment or debrief phase – this digital guide will point the person to trips, courses or resources catered for him or her.
  • CMRA enhanced with personal + aggregated church-level report.
2026
  • Antioch Summit 2026 held at Paya Lebar Methodist Church (1,124 participants from 180 churches, 68 Christian organisations).
  • Bi-Vocational Missions Primer, a strategic resource to help the church understand the concept of bi-vocational missions, was launched.
  • Antioch Incubate, a programme dedicated to building community for those called to long-term missions, was launched.
  • Missions Seed Fund annual report was produced.
  • Missions Training & Education Prospectus, a resource that consolidates missional opportunities across missions agencies, was updated to a live website.

What We Do

Antioch21’s work is structured around three interconnected roles. Each serves a different layer of the missions ecosystem, but all point toward the same goal: helping the Singapore church send missionaries long-term, effectively and sustainably.

Catalyst
Serving the Missions Ecosystem

  • Antioch Summit

    The major gathering of the Singapore missions movement.

    1,124 participants in 2026

  • Prata Meets Tiramisu

    A relational platform for para-churches to connect, share, and align around the shared mission.

    194 participants across 82 organisations

  • Missions Seed Fund

    A catalytic fund supporting smaller churches in sending missionaries, and providing post-field care support.

    30 workers across 11 countries
    Read the Seed Fund report →

  • Bi-vocational Missions Primer

    A primer to develop the Singapore Church’s appreciation of bi-vocational missions.

    Read the Primer →
Champion
Serving the Local Church

  • The Learning Journey

    A monthly training and equipping series for local church leaders.

    51 churches since 2025

  • Church Missions Readiness Assessment (CMRA)

    An online assessment tool to help churches evaluate and strengthen their missions engagement.

    Over 480 unique assessments taken

  • One-on-One Church Engagement

    Direct partnerships with churches to walk alongside their missions development.

Community
Serving Goers and Returned Missionaries

  • Antioch Campus

    A missions exposure programme for youth.

    Over 150 participants across 62 churches

  • 1361

    An equipping track for potential long-term missionaries.

    Over 250 participants from 85 churches

  • Burning Amber

    A safe platform for missionaries to share stories and find encouragement.

    232 participants from 55 churches

  • Returned Missionaries Support

    Meals and a Retreat (in 2025) to honour returned missionaries, and a research study on re-entry, with findings expected in 2027.

Story 1
Worth The Wait
Bethany Trinity Presbyterian’s Missions Journey

For about 20 years, the members of Bethany Trinity Presbyterian Church prayed that God would raise a long-term missionary from their congregation. What they did not expect was that within a few years, He would ask them to send three.

“We prayed for 20 years before we had our own home-grown missionary. Prayer is an exercise in dependency on God.”

Elder Andrew Leong, Missions Committee Chairperson
Story 2
A Family’s Journey
John & Lily (not their real names)

The Missions Seed Fund has brought great encouragement through financial and prayer support to our family as we serve in a creative access region with less than 1% believers.

“In our first year we have witnessed locals being touched by Our Father, in tears when they felt that God loves them; God moving in their hearts as we have deep conversations about the Gospel. Many have had the first chance to hear the Good news.”

John & Lily, serving in a creative access nation
Story 3
From Sender to Goer
Pastor Mit’s Journey to Bangkok

Since July 2026, Pastor Mit, his wife and their two children have made Bangkok their home. Sent by Moulmein Church of Christ, the family serves with a business-as-mission café, and they plan to stay for the long term.

“Let us be known as a church that sends out our best.”

A church leader, Moulmein Church of Christ
